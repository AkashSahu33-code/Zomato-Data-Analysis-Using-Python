Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis
Overview

This project presents a structured analysis of a large-scale restaurant dataset to evaluate pricing strategies, cuisine demand, and regional rating patterns. The objective is to extract meaningful insights from 40,000+ restaurant records and understand the key factors influencing customer preferences and market positioning across multiple cities.

Dataset Description

The dataset contains over 40,000 restaurant records with 15+ attributes, including cuisine type, pricing information, customer ratings, city location, online delivery availability, and service features. The multi-city coverage enables comparative regional analysis and segmentation across diverse market conditions.

Methodology

The analysis begins with comprehensive data preprocessing, including handling missing values, correcting inconsistencies, standardizing categorical variables, and transforming pricing and rating attributes into structured numerical formats. Outlier detection and distribution checks were performed to ensure analytical reliability.

Exploratory data analysis was conducted to examine pricing distributions, cuisine popularity across cities, and the relationship between cost and customer ratings. Correlation analysis and segmentation techniques were applied to identify high-performing categories and optimal pricing tiers. Regional comparisons were performed to evaluate demand variation and rating concentration patterns.

Key Findings

The analysis revealed that mid-range pricing segments demonstrated stronger rating concentration compared to premium categories. Certain cuisine types consistently outperformed others across metropolitan regions. Additionally, restaurants offering online delivery showed improved customer engagement patterns. Significant regional variations were observed in cuisine demand and pricing structures.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates the application of structured data cleaning, statistical analysis, and exploratory modeling to transform raw restaurant data into actionable business insights. The findings support data-driven decision-making related to pricing optimization, expansion strategy, and customer engagement improvement.
